Frequently asked questions

What is Eridian translator?

Eridian is a playful fan-made encoding system that uses two music-note emojis to represent binary digits. 🎵 represents 0, and 🎶 represents 1.

How does the translator work?

Each English letter is represented by six binary digits. 🎵 represents 0, and 🎶 represents 1. The first 26 codes represent uppercase letters A–Z, and the next 26 represent lowercase letters a–z.

Do spaces matter when decoding?

Yes! Each letter must have exactly six consecutive music-note emojis. Do not insert, remove, or change spaces within an encoded sequence. Spaces between words and punctuation from the original message are preserved by the translator. If you manually edit the note sequences, decoding may fail.

Can I use punctuation and special characters?

Spaces, punctuation, numbers, and ordinary symbols are left unchanged. Accented or special letters such as ø, é, and ñ are not supported and should be avoided for reliable results.

Will uppercase and lowercase letters be preserved?

Yes! The six-bit format assigns different codes to uppercase and lowercase letters, so a correctly encoded message decodes with its original capitalization intact.
